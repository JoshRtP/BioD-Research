# Pilot protocol, sampling design, and cost/burden model

## Objectives and decision gates

The pilot must determine whether the provisional metrics are feasible, repeatable, sensitive, auditable, equitable, and safe to use for incentives/claims. It must not be used to market an outcome before analysis is complete.

Advance a metric only if: data completeness and repeatability meet pre-agreed criteria; material discrepancies can be resolved; costs are proportionate; no severe perverse incentive is found; and the technical/rights panels approve its use.

## Sampling design

- **Target:** approximately 60–90 properties across at least Cerrado, Amazon, and Atlantic Forest soy supply sheds, with a funded option to add other biomes where sourcing is material.
- **Strata:** biome/supply shed; farm size; direct/indirect sourcing; baseline habitat proportion/condition; restoration obligation; water risk; and data availability.
- **Include:** group/smallholder pathway and properties near priority habitat/wetlands/rights-sensitive areas, with safeguards for sensitive information.
- **Comparator:** for condition/biological tests, pair or match intervention and comparison areas where ethical/feasible; use repeated measures and record confounders.
- **Power:** a statistician must set sample size after pilot variance and minimum detectable change are defined; do not imply that 60–90 is automatically powered for biological outcomes.

## Phases

| Phase | Activities | Output/gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Ethics/readiness | Consent, rights screen, data agreements, boundaries, source register | No fieldwork without approval |
| 1. Desktop safeguards | Traceability, conversion, legal/protected/rights risk, source reconciliation | Resolve eligibility issues |
| 2. Baseline | Farm records, habitat/condition, soil cover/erosion, water, restoration; biological sub-sample | Complete baseline and cost log |
| 3. Repeatability | Independent re-measurement on ≥20% sample; compare auditors/providers | Agreement/error findings |
| 4. Follow-up | Repeat management annually; condition after appropriate interval; event checks | Sensitivity/trajectory findings |
| 5. Incentive stress test | Test gaming scenarios, missingness, supplier exclusion risk, grievance pathway | Revised metrics/rules |

## QA/QC and analysis

- Pre-register definitions, formulas, strata, exclusions, and analysis.
- Blind independent re-measurement of at least 20%; calculate agreement/error by metric.
- Record imagery/source date, cloud/classification uncertainty, lab QA, observer, season, and protocol deviations.
- Report missingness and attrition by producer segment.
- Compare expected known groups only where ecologically justified; investigate false pass/fail outcomes.
- Require community/rightsholder review of rights-related findings and data handling.

## Cost and burden model template

Capture actual hours and cash cost rather than using unsupported fixed estimates.

| Cost component | Unit | Expected burden band | Cost-reduction option |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boundary/traceability setup | hours/property + central GIS | Medium initially, low recurring | Buyer/group-managed GIS |
| Annual remote sensing | central cost/property | Low–medium | Portfolio procurement and risk triage |
| Farm records/IPM/soil cover | hours/season | Medium | Mobile/offline forms, group support |
| Habitat/restoration field plots | crew-days/property | Medium–high | Stratified sample; photo points |
| Water sampling | samples + travel/lab | High where required | Risk trigger; coordinated basin sampling |
| Biological surveys | survey events + expert analysis | High | Leadership sub-sample only |
| Assurance | auditor-days + travel | Medium–high | Risk-based/group sampling without weakening safeguards |

Analyze costs by biome, farm size, direct/indirect supplier, and metric; separately report program-paid and producer-paid burdens.
