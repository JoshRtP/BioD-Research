## Desk sensitivity analysis

Three plausible weighting perspectives were considered qualitatively against the base weights:

1. **Integrity/ecology emphasis:** raises ecological relevance, auditability, and rights. Safeguards, habitat extent, riparian/wetland protection, and condition remain highest priority; biological-response metrics improve but remain pilot-limited.
2. **Feasibility emphasis:** raises cost, actionability, and maturity. Traceability, conversion, habitat extent, pesticide records, soil cover, and water-risk screens rise; biological response and field freshwater condition fall.
3. **Outcome emphasis:** raises state/response and sensitivity. Habitat condition, restoration condition, connectivity, freshwater condition, and taxa response rise, but their confidence and pilot gates still prevent universal rollout.

**Robust conclusion:** safeguards and M07–M11 remain in the minimum/core architecture under all perspectives. The main decision-sensitive boundary is between conditional core, leadership, and pilot for M13–M18.

## Disagreements that must be elicited, not averaged away

- 2016 versus 2020 or earlier conversion cutoff and whether all natural ecosystems are included.
- Reference ecosystem and acceptable condition trajectory by biome.
- Who bears cost and accountability for landscape metrics.
- Pesticide toxic-load method and acceptable trajectory.
- Data governance for sensitive rights/species data.
- Numeric restoration/condition thresholds and treatment of reversals.
- Claim wording and whether suppliers receive financial/technical support.

## Stakeholder scoring protocol

1. Review safeguards separately and record any objection as a decision issue.
2. Give each participant plain-language specifications and evidence-confidence labels.
3. Score independently before discussion; capture participant group and rationale.
4. Recalculate base and alternative weights; show rank movement and confidence.
5. Report minority/rightsholder views separately; do not use arithmetic consensus to override rights.
6. Update treatment only through a logged steering-committee decision with advisory-panel review.
