# Biodiversity research execution package

**Desk-research release:** 1.0 — 12 June 2026
**Decision status:** Provisional recommendation; ready for Brazilian expert review, stakeholder validation, and pilot execution.

This package executes the repository research plan as far as desk research can credibly go. It does **not** claim that stakeholder workshops, Brazilian legal opinions, farm pilots, or statistical validation have occurred. Those remaining gates are explicit in the roadmap and pilot protocol.

## Working scope and assumptions

Because no approved scope memo was supplied, this release uses a conservative working scope:

- **Commodity:** soy is the core case because RTRS is the only supplied farm production standard; the architecture remains extensible to other crops.
- **Geography:** Brazil; all six continental biomes; initial implementation should prioritize soy supply sheds in Cerrado, Amazon, and Atlantic Forest.
- **Actors/scales:** field, rural property, landscape/supply shed, and sourcing portfolio.
- **Program uses:** eligibility/preferred sourcing, continuous improvement, performance recognition, and carefully bounded public claims. Biodiversity-credit claims are out of scope until separate additionality, leakage, permanence, and benefit-sharing rules are validated.
- **Assurance:** annual first-party monitoring plus risk-based independent third-party verification; failed safeguards cannot be offset by a score.
- **Cutoff:** use the strictest applicable law, buyer commitment, or certification cutoff. For a new program, adopt **31 December 2020 at the latest** for all-natural-ecosystem conversion while preserving earlier RTRS dates where RTRS claims are made.

## Recommended metric set at a glance

### Non-negotiable safeguards

1. Full property and supply-chain traceability.
2. Legal compliance and demonstrable land-use rights; rights grievance and FPIC screen.
3. No natural-ecosystem conversion after the applicable cutoff.
4. Protection of legally protected, riparian, wetland, and priority habitat.
5. Protection of threatened species and prohibition of hunting/collection.
6. Prohibited-pesticide compliance and no contamination/buffer breach.

### Core universal improvement metrics

1. Native/semi-natural habitat extent and annual loss.
2. Habitat-management-plan implementation and condition trajectory.
3. Riparian/wetland protection and restoration progress.
4. Agrochemical toxic-load trajectory and integrated pest management.
5. Year-round soil cover, crop diversity/rotation, and erosion-risk control.
6. Water-risk screening and water-quality incident response.
7. Restoration survival and condition trajectory where restoration is required.

### Conditional/leadership metrics

- Landscape connectivity; freshwater quality; threatened-species action; pollinator or bird occupancy; soil-biota response; invasive-species control; verified outcome claims.

## How to use this package

1. Approve or amend the working scope.
2. Obtain Brazilian legal and rights review before treating the legal crosswalk as authoritative.
3. Use the crosswalk to configure program requirements; do not treat framework guidance as certification equivalence.
4. Run stakeholder scoring and the pilot before fixing numeric improvement thresholds or making outcome claims.
5. Recheck source versions at least annually and before every program release.
